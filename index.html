    Fake "Amazon" Task Website USER-REPORTED · UNVERIFIED LISTING

    shoppinggroup8.com

    Instagram video se ek shakhs ne rabta kiya aur is site par le gaya. Site par Amazon ki branding thi, lekin domain Amazon ka nahi tha. Account me "Recharge", "Withdrawal" aur "Invite task" ke section thay — paisay nikalwane ke liye pehle aur paisay daalne ko kaha jata hai. User ka SadaPay account is se bind ho gaya tha, aur site par account delete ya card unbind karne ka koi tareeqa mojood nahi tha — sirf "customer service se rabta karo", jiska koi contact hi nahi.

    • Bari brand ka naam, lekin website ka domain us brand ka nahi
    • Withdrawal "unlock" karne ke liye recharge / deposit ki shart
    • Recruiter sirf WhatsApp/Instagram par — koi daftar, koi official email nahi
    • Account delete / card unbind ka koi option nahi

    Sahi action: SadaPay card foran freeze/replace karo, mazeed deposit bilkul na karo ("withdrawal unlock" sab se bara jaal hai), recruiter ko block karo, screenshots mehfooz rakho, aur FIA Cybercrime par report karo.

    Pakistan me aam scams — pehchan seekho

    Har scam ka tareeqa alag hai, lekin jaal hamesha aik jaisa hota hai: jaldi karwao, lalach do, aur sochne ka waqt na do. Jo type tumhe nazar aaye, uski nishaniyan yaad kar lo.

    Task / Job Scam BOHOT AAM

    "Ghar bethay roz Rs 2,000–5,000 kamao — sirf products like karo, video dekho, chhote task karo." Shuru me chhoti rakam milti bhi hai taake bharosa bane. Phir bara task aata hai jiske liye pehle apne paisay "recharge" karne parte hain — aur wahi paisay phans jate hain.

    NISHANIYAN
    • Job sirf WhatsApp/Telegram par, koi interview ya daftar nahi
    • Kaam shuru karne ya withdrawal ke liye pehle deposit
    • Website par Recharge / VIP Level / Invite Task jese section

    Fake Investment / Double Paisay BOHOT NUQSAN

    "Rs 10,000 lagao, aik hafte me Rs 20,000 wapas." Crypto, trading ya "online business" ke naam par paisay le kar pehle chhota profit dikhate hain, phir bari rakam lagwa kar ghayab ho jate hain. Asli investment me munafa kabhi guaranteed nahi hota.

    NISHANIYAN
    • Guaranteed ya "double" munafe ka wada
    • Doston ko laane par extra bonus (pyramid wali boo)
    • Paisay personal JazzCash/EasyPaisa number par bhejne ko kehna

    Bank / OTP Call Scam BOHOT AAM

    Call aati hai: "Main bank se bol raha hoon, aapka account band ho raha hai, OTP batayen." Ya "aapke naam par case hai." Darr aur jaldi paida karke OTP, PIN ya card number nikalwa lete hain. Yaad rakho: asli bank, PTA ya police kabhi phone par OTP nahi maangte.

    NISHANIYAN
    • OTP, PIN, password ya card ke aakhri number maangna
    • "Account abhi band ho jayega" jesi dhamki aur jaldi
    • Koi app (AnyDesk / screen-share) install karne ko kehna

    Fake Online Store AAM

    Instagram/Facebook page par iPhone, branded shoes ya makeup adhi se bhi kam qeemat par. Advance payment le kar ya to ghayab, ya nakli/used cheez bhej dete hain. Page naya hota hai, comments band hote hain, aur address sirf "online store" likha hota hai.

    NISHANIYAN
    • Qeemat market se ghair-mamooli kam
    • Sirf advance payment — cash on delivery se inkaar
    • Page chand din purana, reviews/check-in kuch nahi

    SIM Promo / VAS Trap ROZ KA NUQSAN

    SMS aata hai: "Mubarak! Aap ne quiz jeeta, ye app download karo" — asal me ek paid service subscribe ho jati hai jo roz Rs 6–8 balance kaat'ti rehti hai. Balance ahista ahista ghayab hota rehta hai aur pata bhi nahi chalta.

    NISHANIYAN
    • Anjaan SMS me link/app download ka kehna
    • Baghair istemal ke balance kam hona
    • Bachao: Jazz par *6611# se active services dekh kar cancel karo; Ufone par bhi app/helpline se VAS band karwao

    Prize / Lottery Scam AAM

    "Aap ne lucky draw me car / Rs 5 lakh jeet liye hain!" Jis muqable me tum ne hissa hi nahi liya, usme jeetna mumkin nahi. Prize "release" karne ke liye tax, processing fee ya OTP maangte hain — wohi asal jaal hai.

    NISHANIYAN
    • Baghair hissa liye "jeet" ki khabar
    • Prize se pehle fee / tax / OTP ki shart
    • Sirf ajnabi mobile number se rabta, official channel nahi

    Red flags — ye dikhay to ruk jao

    Ek nishani ittefaq ho sakti hai. Do ya zyada ek saath hon to samajh lo jaal bichha hua hai.

    Message / call me

    • "Abhi, foran, sirf aaj" — sochne ka waqt na dena
    • OTP, PIN, password ya CNIC number maangna
    • Paisay milne se pehle paisay bhejne ko kehna
    • "Ye baat kisi ko mat batana" — khamoshi ki shart
    • Ghalat Urdu/English, ajeeb si spelling, screenshot jese "official" letter

    Website / page me

    • Bari brand ka logo, lekin domain doosra (jaise shoppinggroup8.com par "Amazon")
    • Account delete, refund ya rabte ka koi saaf tareeqa nahi
    • Payment sirf personal number / direct transfer par
    • Balance screen par dikhta hai, lekin nikalta kabhi nahi
    • Har kaam ke liye naya "level", nayi fee, naya recharge

    Sunehra usool: Jo paisay nikalwane ke liye pehle paisay maange, wo 100% scam hai. Asli job, asli prize aur asli bank — teeno kabhi aisi shart nahi lagate.

    Scam ho gaya hai? Pehle ye 5 kaam karo

    Ghabrana fitri hai, lekin pehle ghante ke kaam sab se ahem hain. Isi tartib me karo.

    1

    Card / account freeze karo

    SadaPay, JazzCash, EasyPaisa ya bank app se card foran freeze karo. Helpline par call karke block bhi karwao.

    2

    Passwords badlo

    Email, bank aur wallet ke passwords foran badlo — khaas tor par jo password scam site par istemal hua tha.

    3

    Saboot mehfooz karo

    Chats, numbers, website links, payment receipts ke screenshots lo. Chat delete mat karo — yehi tumhara saboot hai.

    4

    Mazeed paisay mat bhejo

    "Withdrawal unlock" ya "aakhri fee" ke naam par aur paisay bhejna nuqsan dugna kar deta hai. Rabta yahin khatam karo.

    5

    Report karo

    FIA Cybercrime par online shikayat darj karo aur number ko block + report karo taake doosre bach saken. Neeche report banane wala tool hai.

    FIA Cybercrime — Helpline
    1991

    Online shikayat: complaint.fia.gov.pk — saboot (screenshots) attach karna na bhoolna.

    PTA — Ghalat SMS / Number ki Shikayat
    0800-256813

    Fraud number ya spam SMS ke liye PTA ki toll-free helpline. PTA ka "DIRBS / spam report" system number block karwane me madad karta hai.

    Apna Bank / Wallet
    Card ke peeche wala number

    Har bank aur wallet (SadaPay, JazzCash, EasyPaisa) ki apni helpline hoti hai. Transaction rokne ki koshish ke liye jitni jaldi call karo, utna behtar.

    Report tayyar karo

    Detail bharo — ek saaf, mukammal shikayat ka text ban jayega jo tum FIA portal par paste kar sakte ho ya WhatsApp par ghar walon / doston ko warning ke tor par bhej sakte ho. Ye text sirf yahin banta hai, kahin save nahi hota.

    Tumhari tafseel

    Jo maloom hai wahi likho — khaali khana bhi chalega.

    Left taraf detail bhar kar "Report ka text banao" dabao — tumhari shikayat yahan ban kar aayegi. Isi text ko: • FIA portal (complaint.fia.gov.pk) par paste karo • Ya doston/ghar walon ko bhej kar unhe isi scam se bachao
